KEY FINDINGS - WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW
===================================================================================

MME (CD10/Neprilysin) IN YOUR DATA:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PSEUDO-BULK ANALYSIS:
  ❌ MME: log2FC = 1.04, p-adj = 0.93 (NOT significant)
  
SINGLE-CELL ANALYSIS: ⭐
  ✅ MME is a STRONG MARKER for cluster 12 (log2FC = 7.57)
  ✅ MME marks cluster 14 which is NEARLY ELIMINATED in treatment:
     • Control: 97 cells
     • Treatment: 4 cells
  ✅ This is a MAJOR finding that pseudo-bulk analysis completely missed!

WHY THE DIFFERENCE?
  • MME is only expressed in ~180 cells (out of 7,692)
  • Pseudo-bulk averages across all cells → signal diluted → not significant
  • Single-cell preserves cell identity → reveals MME+ cells are lost
