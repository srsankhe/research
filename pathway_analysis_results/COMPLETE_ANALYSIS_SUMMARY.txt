━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MME/Mme (NEPRILYSIN/CD10) is a zinc-dependent metalloproteinase that:

🔹 SUBSTRATE SPECIFICITY:
   • Amyloid-beta peptides (Alzheimer's disease)
   • Angiotensins (blood pressure regulation)
   • Substance P, enkephalins (pain signaling)
   • Atrial natriuretic peptide (cardiovascular homeostasis)
   • Various peptide hormones

🔹 IMMUNE FUNCTION:
   • Expressed on neutrophils and immune cells
   • Regulates inflammatory peptides
   • Marker for certain immune cell types
   • Involved in immune cell differentiation

🔹 DISEASE ASSOCIATIONS:
   • Alzheimer's disease (neuroprotective - degrades Aβ)
   • Kidney disease (FSGS, glomerular damage)
   • Cardiovascular disease (RAS system regulation)
   • Cancer (cell surface marker - CD10)
   • Acute kidney injury (IRI model shows changes)

🔹 THERAPEUTIC POTENTIAL:
   • Alzheimer's therapy target (increase activity)
   • Hypertension treatment (ACE inhibitor interactions)
   • Cancer biomarker and potential target
